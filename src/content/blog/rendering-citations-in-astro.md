## Citations

The site renders Pandoc-style citation keys against a BibTeX file. Bioconductor gave genomics a coherent computational ecosystem [@huber2015], and the Gene Expression Omnibus remains a cornerstone public archive [@barrett2013]. Both references resolve to a formatted bibliography below, generated by `rehype-citation` using the APA style.
